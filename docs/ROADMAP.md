# Roadmap

Ideas for after the first release, roughly in order. Vote or suggest more in the GitHub issues.

- **Read your real ship fit from the game:** the Fleet Manager's Loadout Estimate and the Vehicle Loadout
  Manager list what's fitted. A screenshot of either could fill in the Ships tab for you.
- **Rebuild after a claim:** compare a ship's saved fit with what came back from a claim without insurance,
  and list what's missing, where your spares are, and where to buy the rest.
- **Task loadouts:** save more than one fit per ship, and personal (FPS) loadouts, per job (hauling today,
  bounty tomorrow), and see what you have, where, and what's missing.
- **Spot freight elevator changes:** the log records storage volume, but the number shifts with what the kiosk
  is showing, so it can't yet be trusted to say "something changed here, rescan".
- **Reputation-aware tips:** blueprints come from contracts that need standing with a faction. Reading your
  standings would let "how to get it" say which contracts you can take now.
- **Personal gear from the log:** the game logs what you equip, which may let personal loadouts be
  captured automatically.
- **Org view:** load share files from org mates to see who holds what; org invites.
- **Linux:** the dashboard and log tracking should already run; kiosk reading needs screen capture that
  works under Wine/Proton.
- **Single-file download** (an .exe) so nobody needs Python.
- **Other game languages:** tooltip labels like "Item Type" made configurable.
